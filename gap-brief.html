Module 5

The Gap Brief

This brief examines a skill from a target job posting that I wanted to understand more deeply and shows how I would explain that skill in a professional interview.

Target Role

AI & Data Engineering Solutions Delivery Analyst

My target role is an AI & Data Engineering Solutions Delivery Analyst. The position combines data engineering, analytics, automation, and technology consulting.

One line from the job posting that stood out to me was the expectation to design, build, and maintain scalable data systems while supporting high data quality and integration across platforms.

The Gap

What I Wanted to Understand Better

Before researching this topic, I understood the basics of databases, SQL, data analysis, and data cleaning, but I had less experience with what scalable data engineering looks like in a large organization.

I wanted to better understand how data engineers build systems that can reliably collect, transform, store, and deliver data for business users and analytics teams.

What I Learned

Data Engineering in Practice

Data engineering is not only about writing SQL or moving data from one place to another. It involves building reliable processes that allow data to move from source systems into storage, analytics platforms, dashboards, and machine learning workflows.

A strong data pipeline should be repeatable, monitored, scalable, and designed to maintain data quality. Engineers also need to think about performance, security, documentation, failure handling, and how changes in one system may affect another.

This helped me understand why employers value skills in areas such as data transformation, database design, automation, cloud platforms, ETL processes, and data quality.

Connection to My Experience

What I Can Already Defend

My Information Science coursework has given me experience with SQL, database design, Python, R, data analysis, statistics, and machine learning. These skills give me a foundation for working with structured data and understanding how data moves through an analytical workflow.

My LiftAI project also connects to this area because it involves collecting historical workout information, processing the data, generating predictions, and presenting results through an application.

While I am still developing deeper experience with large-scale data systems, I can explain the concepts clearly and show that I already have a strong technical foundation to build from.

Interview Response

How I Would Answer

If I were asked about scalable data systems in an interview, I would explain that the goal is to create a reliable process for moving data from source systems into a form that can be used for analysis, reporting, or machine learning.

I would also explain that scalability means the system should continue to perform effectively as data volume, users, or business requirements increase.

I would connect this to my experience with Python, SQL, database design, data cleaning, and analytics, while being clear that I am continuing to build experience with larger production data environments.

What This Demonstrates

Research & Professional Growth

This brief demonstrates my ability to identify a gap in my current knowledge, research the topic, connect it to my existing experience, and turn that learning into a stronger interview response.

It also shows that I can look at a job posting critically and use employer expectations to guide the skills I continue developing.
